Frequently Asked Questions

Does it cost anything to use a travel agent for Disney, Universal, or a cruise?

No. My planning services are free to you, and I never charge a planning fee. I'm compensated by my travel partners, and pricing is set by the resort, park, or cruise line.

Can you book Disney vacation packages?

Yes. I plan Walt Disney World and Disneyland vacations, including resort selection, park strategy, Lightning Lane planning, and dining reservations. I'm a Disney College of Knowledge graduate.

Are you a Disney Cruise Line travel agent?

Yes. I plan Disney Cruise Line sailings, including cabin category selection, embarkation-day planning, and shore excursions.

Can you book Royal Caribbean and other cruise lines?

Yes. I book Royal Caribbean, Celebrity, Norwegian, MSC, Carnival, and Disney Cruise Line. I hold training credentials with Royal Caribbean and Norwegian Cruise Line (NCLU Master's Degree).

Do you book Universal Orlando and Universal Studios Hollywood?

Yes. I'm a Universal Orlando Resort Specialist and a Universal Studios Hollywood Specialist. I help with ticket options, Express Pass strategy, and on-site hotel perks.

Do you only work with travelers near Franklin, Indiana?

No. I'm based in Franklin, Indiana, and I plan trips for travelers anywhere in the United States by email and phone.

How does planning with you work, and how fast will I hear back?

Fill out the free quote form with your dates, destination, and who's traveling. I typically respond within 24 hours, build an itinerary and budget around your group, handle the reservations, and stay available during your trip.

Who is behind A Bit of Pixie Travel?

A Bit of Pixie Travel is the personal brand of Carey Richey, an independent travel advisor based in Franklin, Indiana and affiliated with Best Day Ever Vacations.
