How Disney vacation packages work

A Disney vacation package bundles a resort stay with park tickets, and sometimes extras like dining. What is offered and what is included change by season, so I confirm the current options for your exact dates before you decide. I can also build a trip without a package if that fits your budget better.

Who I plan for

Most of my clients are families with kids, couples celebrating something, and small groups traveling together. If it's your first Disney trip, I'll keep the plan simple. If you've been before, I'll focus on what you want to do differently this time.
